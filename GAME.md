## The toolbox

> Four things see the most use.

- [colony](https://github.com/Dhodraj/colony) — runs several Claude Code sessions in parallel, one task each, through a shared log.
- [seekly](https://github.com/Dhodraj/seekly) — searches a codebase in plain language, from the terminal.
- [collectweak](https://github.com/Dhodraj/collectweak) — typed, chainable extensions to `Map` and `Set`, no runtime deps.
- [deep-freeze-utility](https://github.com/Dhodraj/deep-freeze-utility-pkg) — freezes nested objects, recursively.

## How he decides

> A card is pinned above the desk. Six lines, in order.

1. First principles. Understand the mechanism underneath.
2. Second-order. Follow the consequences downstream.
3. Inverse. Find the failure modes, and what not to build.
4. Simplicity. The simplest thing that meets the real need.
5. Evidence. Research, measure, verify.
6. Goals. Outcomes over technology fashion.
